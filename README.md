# Melfie's Digital Support

Create a clean, modern, professional one-page personal portfolio website for Melfie James Antonio, a recent BS Computer Science graduate who is seeking opportunities as a Virtual Assistant and Administrative/Digital Support professional. The website should feel trustworthy, organized, approachable, and professional rather than looking like a traditional programmer portfolio. Use a modern responsive layout with a polished but simple visual design, subtle animations, rounded cards, good spacing, and excellent mobile responsiveness. The hero section should prominently display “Melfie James Antonio” and “Virtual Assistant | Administrative & Digital Support,” with a short introduction explaining that I provide reliable support with email management, data entry, web research, Canva content, document organization, and other digital administrative tasks. Add clear buttons for “View My Work” and “Contact Me.” Create a Services section featuring Email Management, Data Entry & Spreadsheet Management, Canva & Basic Graphic Design, Web Research, File & Document Organization, Calendar & Task Management, and Basic Technical Support. Create a Portfolio/Sample Work section containing realistic mock projects such as an organized email inbox, a professional Canva social media graphic, a cleaned and organized spreadsheet, a web research report, a professionally formatted document, and a simple task-management/dashboard example; clearly label these as sample projects rather than claiming they are client work. Add an About Me section explaining that I am a BS Computer Science graduate with experience in technology, troubleshooting, web development, research, and digital tools, and that I am a reliable and persistent learner who is willing to learn new tools and workflows. Add a Tools section featuring Canva, Google Workspace, Microsoft Office, Excel/Google Sheets, and common productivity/task-management tools. Keep programming and technical skills as supporting skills rather than the main focus. Add a simple Contact section with a professional contact form and space for my email and social/profile links. Include a downloadable Resume button. Make the entire website feel like a real beginner VA's professional portfolio that could be sent directly to potential clients, avoiding exaggerated claims, fake testimonials, fake client logos, or invented work experience. Use placeholder content where personal contact information or portfolio files are not yet provided, so I can replace them later. Ensure the site is fully responsive, accessible, fast-loading, and visually polished.
